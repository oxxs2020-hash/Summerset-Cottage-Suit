The King Suite

Douglas, Michigan
2 Guests 1 Bedroom 1 Bath 5.0 Top-Rated on Airbnb (39 Reviews)

A Sophisticated Escape

Kick back and relax in this stylish, newly built property's King guest suite featuring a private entrance. Designed for ultimate comfort and relaxation, the large second-floor King Suite has been thoughtfully curated with a small kitchenette, a cozy fireplace, and a beautiful light-filled sitting area.

Strategically located on the edge of the city limits of Douglas, the suite overlooks a sweeping meadow. It is positioned perfectly for a very short walk to both Lake Michigan and downtown Douglas.

Step outside into a true paradise. The entire property features professional, high-end landscaping, enveloped by spectacular, god-like beautiful flowers and lush plants that create a serene, luxurious oasis for your stay.
